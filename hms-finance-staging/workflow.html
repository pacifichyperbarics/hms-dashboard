How Payables Works

One understandable path from a possible bill to cash planning and the P&L
FinanceSetupCurrent Payables
The central rule: finding a bill, approving a bill, paying a bill, and posting an expense are four different events. The system records each one separately so the P&L never relies on a checkbox or an assumption.

Daily operating workflow

1Find

Email, upload, manual entry, recurring schedule, vendor notice, or bank activity identifies something that may need attention.

2Inbox

The item lands in one place. It is not yet approved, paid, or posted. Duplicates and missing information are flagged.

3Review and code

Confirm vendor, amount, due date, invoice, clinic, category, supporting document, and how the vendor is normally paid.

4Authorize

Michael checks the box, or an approved automatic rule passes its amount and destination limits. Authorization still does not move money.

5Pay and verify

The payment recipe gives the method. A bank withdrawal later confirms what actually happened.

↓
6Reconcile

Match the bank transaction to the payable. Split, combined, timing, reversal, and unmatched cases go to review.

7Post accounting

Only a confirmed, coded reconciliation creates a balanced journal entry. Posted accounting cannot be silently edited.

8P&L

The P&L reads posted journal lines. It does not read an Inbox item or an approval checkbox.

9Cash requirements

Open bills, approved bills, payroll, CAPEX, transfers, expected receipts, and current cash create the forward cash plan.

Cost-reduction loop

Subscriptions and recurring spend are scanned separately for cancellation, reduction, consolidation, annual-billing savings, price increases, unused seats, and negotiation opportunities.

A savings suggestion is not an accounting entry. The forecast may show expected savings, but the P&L changes only after actual spending changes.

What the important actions mean

Add to Inbox“This might be a bill. Please review it.” Nothing is authorized.
Authorize checkbox“This bill may be paid.” It does not mean the payment happened.
Automatic“Approve only when the known vendor, normal amount range, and verified payment destination all match.”
Bank confirmed“The money actually left the account.” This is the evidence used for reconciliation.
Posted“The balanced accounting entry is now part of the P&L.”
Hold / exception“Do not proceed until duplicate, coding, invoice, amount, or payment-instruction questions are resolved.”
